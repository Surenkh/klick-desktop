## Klick 0.6.0 — 16 real keyboard packs, all MIT or CC0

Native macOS, 9 MB, ~2–4 ms key-to-ear, no Electron.

### Install in one line

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Surenkh/klick-desktop/main/scripts/install.sh | sh
```

### Or download the DMG

| Mac | File |
| --- | --- |
| Apple silicon (M1–M6) | `Klick-0.6.0-arm64.dmg` |
| Intel | `Klick-0.6.0-x64.dmg` |

### Sixteen packs of real switches

Twelve switches from [tplai/kbsim](https://github.com/tplai/kbsim) (**MIT**, © Thomas
Lai): Alpaca, Gateron Ink Black, Blue Alps, Box Navy, Buckling Spring, NovelKeys
Cream, Holy Panda, Cherry MX Black, Cherry MX Brown, Gateron Red Ink, Topre, Kailh
Pro Turquoise.

Two from [CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds](https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds)
(**CC0 1.0**, Ben Burnes / Abstraction): *Abstraction* and *Abstraction Model D*.

Two from [unicaegames](https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes)
(**CC0 1.0**, Cherry KC 1000 through a Shure SM7B): *Cherry KC 1000* and *Cherry
Typing*.

Nothing is synthesised; no Mixkit or Epidemic Sound audio remains in the app, the
repository, or its history.

### Also in this release

- Pack library: two-column cards with category filters and a three-key audition on
  every click.
- Per-pack licence, author and source in `assets/packs/manifest.json`.
- The withdrawn 0.5.0 release is gone: its studio pack came from Epidemic Sound
  recordings, which cannot be redistributed.
