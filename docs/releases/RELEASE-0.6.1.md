## Klick 0.6.1 — the original sounds are back

Native macOS, 8.8 MB, ~2–4 ms key-to-ear.

### Install in one line

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Surenkh/klick-desktop/main/scripts/install.sh | sh
```

### What's new

- **20 packs.** The four original Klick sounds (`laptop`, `hard`, `fast`, `soft`) are
  back and listed first, next to the sixteen MIT/CC0 switch packs.
- **Tighter samples.** Keys are capped at 140 ms, delete at 200 ms, enter at 240 ms
  and space at 260 ms, each with a 3 ms fade — the sound stops when the click does
  instead of ringing on.
- **Sidebar** puts the Klick mark directly under the window border with the section
  list right beneath it.

### Licensing, plainly

Sixteen packs are MIT or CC0. The four originals are cut from Mixkit recordings under
the Mixkit Free License: free to use inside a project, not to be redistributed as
standalone audio, and **not MIT**. They are bundled here as app content and documented
in `ASSET-LICENSES.md`. If you need a purely MIT/CC0 audio tree, delete those four
folders.
