## Character art — Kiki

Supplied by the project owner as `Klick_App_Assets.zip` (v1.0): seven static
states plus a blink frame at 1254² RGBA, and two animated WebP loops at 512² /
30 fps. The pack's own README states the frames were produced with an image
generation tool and the loops assembled with FFmpeg; the reproducible encoding
script (`build_klick.py`) is kept in `incoming/visuals/Klick_App_Assets/`.

The uploaded black silhouette form of Kiki is used for the macOS menu-bar
template icon (`assets/trayTemplate.png`, generated by
`scripts/make-tray-icon.py`); Windows uses a coloured 32 px crop
(`assets/tray-win.png`) because the taskbar does not tint template images.

`scripts/import-visuals.py` maps Klick's states onto the app's, trims padding,
downscales to 512² and rebuilds the app icon from the idle pose. The art is
distributed with this app with the owner's permission; it is not covered by the
MIT licence on the code, so keep the credit if you fork this.
